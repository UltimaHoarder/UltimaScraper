# Code of Conduct

UltimaScraper follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere you represent the project.

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or
  identity.
- Give and take criticism of code and ideas, not of people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised comments or personal attacks.
- Never post someone else's private information, including account details or
  content from the sites UltimaScraper archives.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainers through the
repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, and say it is a
conduct report. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments,
close threads, or block people who break it.
